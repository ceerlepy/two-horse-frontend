Two Horse is a real-time, AI-powered analysis app for Turkish horse racing.

What it offers:
• Today's TJK race card, live AGF and AGF movements
• A combined view built from many expert sources
• A score for every horse, plus recent runs, training and gallop data
• Budget-based sixfold (altılı) and fivefold (beşli) coupon suggestions
• Foreign race cards
• Turkish and English interface

Two Horse is not a betting site; it does not take bets or pay out. The analysis is for information only and does not guarantee any winnings. The app is for users aged 18 and over. Please play responsibly.
